THE PIT STOP UNIVERSE

Every story starts somewhere.

Four little Mechanix. A world much bigger than the workshop they were built for.

From their beginnings in a laboratory to the garage they eventually call home, the Mechanix are discovering what it means to create, explore and belong. Their adventures are being developed into a connected world of stories, characters and everyday workshop life.

BOOK ONE · ORIGIN STORY

Everything in Its Place

A brilliant but eccentric Scientist creates four mechanical assistants to help build an ambitious spacecraft. Nutz is his first creation, but his inventive nature proves rather less predictable than expected. Learning from that first experiment, the Scientist creates Sparkz, Torx and Wheelz, each bringing different strengths to the project.

As the Mechanix develop friendships and discover a fascination with motorsport, they begin imagining a future of their own. What starts with curiosity leads to a secret workshop, their first little racing car and a journey that eventually brings them to The Pit Stop Garage. There, they find more than somewhere to work. They find a home, a family and a place where they can finally enjoy doing what they love.

The Origin Story is structured across four acts and fifteen chapters, following the Mechanix from creation and awakening through difficult choices to finding somewhere they belong.

FEATURED GAME CONCEPT

TPS Garage

Manage, organise, keep the repairs flowing and the customers happy.

Take charge of The Pit Stop Garage in a run-based workshop-management puzzle. Accept customer jobs, keep the Mechanix supplied, assemble parts and get cars repaired before their deadlines.

Components arrive from Nutz's machine and travel along a limited conveyor. You'll need to manage the flow, deliver materials to the right specialists and keep the workshop moving while avoiding waste, delays and costly mistakes.

As the garage develops, invest in permanent upgrades, improve your crew and introduce robot helpers to automate routine work. Expand your workshop, unlock new capabilities and eventually purchase larger garages with more repair bays and room to grow. Each run gives you another opportunity to improve your operation and take on more demanding jobs.
