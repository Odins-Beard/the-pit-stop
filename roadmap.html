THE PIT STOP · ROADMAP

The Road
Ahead.

The Pit Stop has come a long way from its beginnings as a community of car and gaming enthusiasts.

Along the way we've created our NFT collection, introduced the Mechanix and begun building a much bigger world around them.

Now, as part of the Highground Studios family, we're moving forward into the next chapter of The Pit Stop.

This roadmap gives an overview of what we're working towards. It isn't intended to be a fixed schedule — ideas will evolve, priorities may change and new opportunities will appear along the way.

WHAT WE'RE WORKING TOWARDS

The next chapter.

Collections, characters, stories and games — different parts of The Pit Stop, gradually coming together.

01

COLLECTIONS

Bringing the Collection Back to Life

We're giving holders new ways to explore and enjoy their collections, beginning with a wallet-connected collection viewer.

We'll also be restoring and improving existing parts of the ecosystem, including minting, while exploring the next steps for TPS Scraps and staking.

02

THE MECHANIX

Bringing the Crew to Life

Sparkz, Torx, Wheelz and Nutz have always been at the heart of The Pit Stop. We want to develop their personalities, specialisms and stories further, while creating new ways for visitors to interact with them.

Over time, that will include genuine AI-powered Mechanix, allowing the crew to become much more than characters on a page.

And perhaps there might even be a new face around The Pit Stop Garage...

03

STORIES

Expanding the World

There is much more to The Pit Stop than the garage doors currently reveal.

We'll begin bringing the world we've been developing to an audience through storyboards, previews and eventually our first proper story releases — exploring the origins of The Pit Stop, the Mechanix and the wider world around them.

04

GAMING

Built to Play

Games have always been part of the DNA of The Pit Stop. We're exploring experiences ranging from smaller games inspired by the existing collection through to larger concepts such as TPS Garage.

We'll also continue to explore opportunities for The Pit Stop within other gaming worlds and platforms where they make sense.

05

THE LIVING PIT STOP

A Garage That Feels Alive

Our longer-term vision is to bring the different parts of The Pit Stop together — the Mechanix, stories, games, collections and interactive experiences — into a world that feels increasingly alive.

Somewhere to explore, discover things and spend a little time with the crew. Not simply a website to read, but a place that feels like The Pit Stop Garage is carrying on with its day whether you're there or not.

ALWAYS EVOLVING

The road isn't fixed.

The Pit Stop has always grown through ideas, experimentation and the people around it.

Some things on this roadmap will evolve as we build them, and we're sure a few unexpected ideas will find their way into the workshop too.

That's part of the fun.
