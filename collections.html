RARITY & PERFORMANCE

There's more to a car than its rarity

Every vehicle combines rarity with its own performance characteristics.

RARITY

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Legendary → Mythic

Higher rarity generally brings stronger overall performance, but every model has its own strengths and weaknesses.

Some cars sit towards the top of their rarity class while others fall towards the bottom, with performance sometimes overlapping the next class.

In other words: watch out for the supers... and the lemons.

VEHICLE STATS

Four attributes, different strengths

Every NFT has four underlying attributes:

Acceleration · Top Speed · Handling · Prestige

Each individual stat has a maximum value of 5, giving every vehicle its own balance of performance and prestige.

MECHANIX-TUNED

Some cars have had a little extra attention

Selected vehicles have been worked on by The Pit Stop Mechanix, giving them special changes to their performance.

THE SPECIALISTS

Tuned for performance

THE OG COLLECTION

Where it all began

The Pit Stop OG Passes were a one-time collection of just 100 numbered passes created for our earliest supporters.

The collection sold out and remains a permanent part of Pit Stop history.

The first 50 holders also had the opportunity to receive a bespoke car NFT of their choice.

As TPS moves forward, OG ownership will continue to be recognised alongside holder rank.
